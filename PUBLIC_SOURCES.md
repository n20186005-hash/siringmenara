- [Banjarmasin Satu Data, tourism objects 2024](https://satudata.banjarmasinkota.go.id/data-statistik/ade9b280-e411-460e-b580-18b9b053a172): separately lists Menara Pandang and Pasar Terapung Siring Tendean; tower address Jl. Kapten Piere Tendean No.07, RT.16/RW.2, Gadang, Banjarmasin Tengah, 70123.
- [City government, 16 January 2025](https://www.banjarmasinkota.go.id/2025/01/elevator-siring-menara-pandang-siap.html): elevator inauguration/testing; operating arrangements were to be determined. A historical report, not current universal lift or step-free access assurance.
- [Disbudporapar, 13 April 2025](https://disbudporapar.banjarmasinkota.go.id/2025/04/lantai-4-rooftop-menara-pandang-sudah.html): historical rooftop reopening and lift schedule. The guide does not reproduce the old timetable as today's hours.
- [Google Maps place](https://maps.app.goo.gl/NifHMqhQZsxkgSTN6): checked snapshot 4.5/5 from 8,016 third-party reviews; pin -3.3169893, 114.5931235. No operating hours were displayed; no verified operator website or telephone is asserted.

The 2018 tourism timetable and later national-tourism summaries are not used as a current operating guarantee. Tower, rooftop, elevator, pedestrian access, market, parking, boats and events require separate confirmation. No free-entry, night-entry, exact current fees, fixed performances or scheduled boat claims are published. No rating, free-access or opening-hours schema is published.
